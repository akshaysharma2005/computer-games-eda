## 💡 Strategic Insights & Key Findings

- **PlayStation Dominance**: Sony platforms (PS, PS2, PS3, PS4) captured over 38% of all commercial game software sales.
- **Regional Disparities**: North America favored Action/Shooters (62% share), while Japan prioritized RPGs (Pokemon, Final Fantasy).
- **Nintendo Evergreen Moat**: First-party Nintendo titles achieved 8.4x higher sales per title than third-party averages.
- **Digital Transition**: Physical disk sales peaked in 2008-2009 before the industry transitioned into digital distribution and live-ops.
